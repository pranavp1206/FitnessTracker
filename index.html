HEALTH TIPS

Simple habits that make a big difference

💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Hydration improves performance, aids recovery, and keeps your metabolism running efficiently.
Nutrition
😴
Prioritise Sleep
Aim for 7–9 hours per night. Sleep is when your muscles repair and grow. Poor sleep spikes cortisol and sabotages fat loss.
Recovery
🥦
Eat Whole Foods
Build meals around vegetables, lean proteins, and complex carbs. Minimise ultra-processed foods for sustained energy and better body composition.
Nutrition
🧘
Manage Stress
Chronic stress increases cortisol, leading to fat storage and cravings. Try 10 minutes of mindfulness or deep breathing daily.
Mindset
🏃
150 Min / Week
The WHO recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity per week. That's just 22 minutes a day — totally achievable!
Activity
🍽️
Don't Skip Breakfast
A protein-rich breakfast jump-starts your metabolism, stabilises blood sugar, and reduces cravings throughout the day.
Nutrition
🦵
Stretch Daily
5–10 minutes of stretching improves flexibility, reduces injury risk, and promotes circulation. Your future self will thank you.
Recovery
🎯
Set SMART Goals
Goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound are far more likely to be reached than vague intentions.
Mindset
🏋️
Strength Train
Lifting weights 2–3× per week builds lean muscle, boosts metabolism, strengthens bones, and improves insulin sensitivity.
